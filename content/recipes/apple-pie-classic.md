## Ingredients

* 6-7 Granny smith apples
* 1 stick butter
* 1/4 cup water
* 3 tablespoons flour
* 3/4 cup sugar
* Spices
* 1.5 teaspoons Cinnamon
* 1 teaspoon Ginger
* 1/2 teaspoon Nutmeg
* Vanilla

## Instructions

* Prepare pie crust beforehand. [Options Here](/tags/pie-crust/)

### Prepare apples

* Peel apples
* Core apples
* thinly slice apples and place itno bowl
* Half a portion of the total sugar used for filling and sprinkle over the apple bowl, mix well
* Wait one hour
* Drain all liquid into another container, use this for sauce.
* Add cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, and any other desired spices to the apples, stir to coat evenly.

### Sauce
* Medium saucepan Medium heat
* Melt 8 tablespoons butter
* 3 tablespoons flour
* Simmer for about a minute and stir constantly
* 1/4 cup water (Use apple water from above)
* 3/4 cup sugar
* Combine, bring to boil, reduce heat, simmer ~3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
* Consider adding a splash of vanilla.
* Should be a light caramel sauce consistency. Remove from heat and let sit for rest of recipe.

### Prepare Pie

* Add sauce to apple bowl, mix well.
* Roll one pie crust into 12 inches and the other to 9 inches
* Lay 12 inches into tin
* Add apples and scrape all sauce into pan. Careful not to get any on the metal or overhang section.

* Take the other pie crust and form it into a circle the same size as the pie tin.
* Cut into 10 even strips.
* Take long strip and place across middle, place a total of 5 strips horizontally.
* Peel back the [0,1,2,3,4] 1 and 3 strips, place a vertical strip underneath in the center.
* Now peel back 0 2 4 and place another. Then 1 3 and again. Then repeat for the other side.
* Use remaining dough scraps to patch edges.
* Fold edges inwards for a folded edge.
* Crimp with fingers. (View Natasha 4:40)

### Egg wash

* Combine 1 egg and 1 tablespoon sugar
* Brush over exposed dough

### Bake

* Bake 15 minutes at 425f.
* Then 45 minutes at 350f, stop if you see thick juices bubbling through the lattice for 5 minutes.
* Remove at let rest for 1 hour.

## Vegan alterations

* Crisco and oat milk
* Crust: 2 sticks of butter = 200g crisco (1 cup)
* Filling: 100g crisco, 1-2 tbsp plant milk, extra vanilla for flavor
* Oat milk instead of egg wash
