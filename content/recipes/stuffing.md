## Ingredients  

* Italian bread
* Onions
* Celery
* Carrot
* Sage
* Thyme
* Rosemary
* Salt
* Pepper
* Chicken Broth

## Instructions  

###

* Let bread go stale or dry out in a warm oven
* Chop vegetables
* Cut bread into cubes
* Combine into large bowl
* Add 1-2 cups of broth at a time until everything is moist but not soggy
* Bake 350f covered 30-40 minutes, remove covering in last 10 minutes
