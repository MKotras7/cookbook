## Ingredients  

### Pork

* 4 thick cut, bone in pork chops
* 1.5 cups kosher salt
* 4 quarts water

# Spice Rub

* 1 tbsp dark brown sugar
* 1 tsp paprika
* 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
* 1/2 tsp ground coriander
* 1 tsp ground cumin
* few cracks black pepper

### Barbeque Sauce

* 1/2 cup ketcup
* half small white onion, grated
* 1 1/2 tbsp worcestershire sauce
* 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
* apple cider vinegar
* 2 tbsp dijon mustard
* 1 tbsp dark brown sugar

## Instructions  

### Brine

* Combine water and salt in a container
* Submerge Pork
* Cover and sit for 1-5 hours

### Spice Rub

* Combine all ingredients

### Barbeque sauce

* Add all ingredients
* Optionally simmer for 30+ minutes

### Pork

* Remove pork chops and dry with paper towels
* Rub with spice
* Cook with vegetable oil to 145f internal
* Remove and clean pan
* Brush on barbeque sauce 
* Cook again to brown sauce and maintain 145f internal
