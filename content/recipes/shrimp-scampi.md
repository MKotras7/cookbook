## Ingredients  

### Pasta

* 1 lb Linguini
* 3 tbps unsalted butter
* 3 tbps olive oil

### Shrimp

* 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
* 4 cloves crushed garlic
* 1/2 cup dry white wine
* 1 lb deveined shrimp
* 1 large lemon
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* 3 tbps parsley, chopped

## Instructions  

### Pasta

* Boil water
* Cook linguini until it still has a bite but you don't need to pick it out of your teeth
* Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water
* Combine with butter and oil

### Shrimp

* Saute red pepper flakes and garlic in oil until fragrant
* Deglaze with white wine
* Simmer until evaporated, about 5 minutes
* Add shrimp
* Cook until shrimp is pink but not cooked through, about 3-4 minutes

### Combine

* Add pasta to shrimp pan
* Add juice of lemon
* Add salt and pepper
* Add 1/4 cup pasta water or more until sauce looks smooth
* Add olive oil
* Sauce should be thick and creamy, cook down or add pasta water 
* Add parsley and any other desired seasonings

## Extras

* Use extra garlic
* Dry brine shrimp, see binging with babish video
* 1 tbps marjoram
* 1 tbps tarragon
