## Ingredients  

* 2 quart chicken stock  
* 1 cup wild rice  
* 1 1/2 chicken breast
* 1 cup celery, diced  
* 1 cup carrots, diced  
* 1/2 cup onion, chopped  

* 2 bay leaves  
* 1 tsp dried parsley  
* 3 cloves garlic, minced  
* 1/4 tsp dried thyme  

**Roux**  

* 1/4 cup butter  
* 1/4 cup all‑purpose flour  
* 1 cup half‑and‑half
* 1 cup milk  

## Instructions  

### Prepare  

1. Season the chicken breasts with salt and pepper.  
2. Place them in a slow cooker.  
3. Add everything else

### Cook the Soup  

1. Cover and cook on low for 6–8 hours. Less for high heat
2. Remove the bay leaves.  
3. Take out the chicken breasts, shred them with two forks, and return the shredded chicken to the slow cooker.

### Make the Roux  

1. In a medium saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat.  
2. Add the flour, whisk until combined, cook until slightly browned.  
3. Slowly add liquids while mixing
4. Cook, whisking frequently, until the mixture thickens (about 5 minutes).  

### Combine and Serve  

1. Stir roux into soup until combined
2. Adjust seasonings to taste
