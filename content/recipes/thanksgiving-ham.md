## Ingredients  

* Bone-in, spiral cut, fully cooked ham

## Instructions  

###

* Remove ham from container
* Place in baking with high walls
* Cover with foil
* Bake at 275-300
* Add water to the bottom of pan
* 10-12 minutes per pound, approximately 2 hours
* Target 140f internal
* Uncover and make glaze according to packet
