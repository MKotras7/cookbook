## Ingredients  

* 1 stick unsalted butter
* 1 2/3rd cup powdered sugar OR 1 cup granulated sugar
* Approximately 1tbsp molasses, guess
* 1 large egg
* Up to 1tsp kosher salt
* 1 tsp baking soda
* Up to 2tsp vanilla extract
* 1 1/2 cups bread or all purpose flour
* 3/4th cup chocolate chips



## Instructions  

* Preheat to 375f
* Brown one stick of butter
* Add sugar, molasses
* Combine and wait to cool down to not cook an egg
* Add egg and beat until dissolved into a pudding consistency, runny if hot
* add salt, baking soda, vanilla extract
* Add flour
* Add some water so it's stick to the touch but doesn't come up with your finger
* Add chocolate chips
* Lay onto baking sheets in taller rounds
* Turn on broiler and wait 2 minutes to heat
* Put cookies in for up to 90 seconds for golden brown domes
* Remove cookies, turn off boilet, wait 2 minutes to cool down
* Set oven to 365f and bake for up to 8 minutes, rotate pan half way through
