## Ingredients

* 200g White rice
* 330g Ground beef
* 330g Carrots
* 330g cucumber
* Sriracha
* Ponzu sauce
* Lime
* Onion
* Scallions

## Notes

This recipe made approximately 3 servings.

beef:carrot:cucumber:rice ratio is approximately 1:1:1:2

## Instructions

### Rice

* Cook rice with a pinch of salt.
* Add 1 tbsp butter, scallion greens, and lime zest.

### Cucumber

* Slice cucumber into slim half moons.
* In a sealable tupperware, combine cucumber, juice from 1/2 lime, 1/4 tsp sugar, and a pinch of salt. 
* Shake frequently to pickle.

### Carrots

* Grate or julienne carrots for desired texture.

### Beef

* Cook diced onion and scallion whites in a pan.
* Add beef, garlic, and 2 tsp sugar. Cook until the beef is browned.
* Add ponzu sauce, salt, and pepper to taste.

### Topping

* Mix 2 tsp mayo with a squeeze of lime juice, a pinch of garlic, sriracha, salt, and pepper for a delicious topping.

### Combine and serve
