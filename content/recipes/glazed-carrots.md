## Ingredients  

* Carrots or Baby Carrots
* 2-3 tbps butter
* 1-2 tbps brown sugar or honey
* Salt, Pepper
* Cinnamon, nutmeg, fresh herbs

## Instructions  

###

* Steam until quite tender, 5-7 minutes or so, 5 or less for baby carrots.
* Pan, melt butter, add sweetener and salt and pepper
* Toss carrots in for 3-5 minutes until coated
* Toss extra spices if desired
