## Ingredients  

* 4 Skinless chicken breast halves (thickness halved)
* 2 Large eggs
* 1 cup panko crumbs
* 3/4 cup grated parmesan cheese
* 2 tbsp flour
* 1/2 cup olive oil
* 1/2 cup marinara sauce
* 1/4 cup fresh mozarella
* 1/4 tbsp chopped basil
* 2 teaspoons oil

## Instructions  

### Bread crumb mixture

* Add bread crumbs, salt, pepper, paprika, parmesan to a bowl

### Chicken Breasts

* Butterfly cut chicken breasts
* Cover with parchment paper and pound smooth and thin
* Bread the chicken breasts in a flour bowl, egg, panko
* Lay on sheet and press extra crumbs into chicken

### Spaghetti

* Cook normally
* Season with olive oil, salt, pepper, parsley, basil leaves
* Some sauce

### Cook Chicken

* Pan fry with a small layer of oil riding up the sides
* 2.5 minutes each side
* Add some butter half way through when edges are cooked
* Place onto sheet
* Once all pieces are cooked on sheet, turn broiler on
* Add marinara sauce and mozarella cheese slices
* Place into broiler until melted
