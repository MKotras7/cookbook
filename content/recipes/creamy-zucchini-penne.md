## Ingredients

* One zucchini
* Garlic
* One lemon
* Italian seasoning
* Penne
* Cream cheese
* Chicken stock
* Parmesan

## Instructions

### Cook Penne

### Bake zucchini at 450F with oil and italian seasoning

### Prepare Sauce

1. Heat 2 tablespoons of butter in a saucepan.
2. Add minced garlic and cook until fragrant.
3. Stir in cream cheese, chicken stock, grated Parmesan, and pasta water.
4. Season with salt and pepper.
