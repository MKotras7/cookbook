## Ingredients  

* 10 Large eggs
* 3/4 Cups sugar
* 1 1/2 cups brandy (korbel)
* 1 1/2 cups rye whiskey
* 1 quart whole milk
* 2 cups heavy cream
* 2-3 tsp ground nutmeg

## Instructions  

### Mix

* Separate egg whites and yolks
* Beat yolks until pale yellow
* Add 1/3rd of the sugar at a time and beat until all combined
* You can continue to add sugar until it stops dissolving. Drizzling from wick should leave a ribbon puddle sort of thing.

* Add brandy and whiskey slowly while stirring, then milk and heavy cream
* Stir in the ground nutmeg, it will not perfectly dissolve, keep mixing until its good enough.

### Foam

* In a separate bowl, whisk egg whites until soft peaks.
* Add 1/3rd of it at a time into main bowl and gently fold
* Let rest at least 4 hours, 2-3 days.
