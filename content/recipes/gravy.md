## Ingredients  

* Ham pan drippings
* 2-3 Tablespoons flour per cup of drippings
* Salt
* Pepper
* Thyme
* Sage

## Instructions  

###

* Let pan drippings sit for a few minutes for fat to separate
* Skim off fat
* Heat drippings in pan to boil
* Add flour
* Cook 1-2 minutes
* Gradually add stock
* Simmer until thick
* Season as you go
