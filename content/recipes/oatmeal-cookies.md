## Ingredients  

* 1 1/2 cups / 188g all purpose flour
* 1 teaspoon cinnamon
* 1 teaspoon baking soda
* 3/4 teaspoon salt
* 1 cup / 2 sticks / 230g unsalted butter
* 1/2 cup / 100g granulated sugar
* 2 large eggs
* 1 tablespoon molasses
* 2 teaspoon vanilla
* 3 cups / 255g oats
* 1 3/4 / 250g cups chocolate chips

## Instructions  

* Whisk dry ingredients except oats and chocolate
* Mix wet ingredients
* Combine wet and dry
* Add oats and chocolate
* chill in fridge to avoid spreading
* Scoop
* Bake 350f 13 minutes
